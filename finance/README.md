# Finance

Notes about money rules, written in words.

Put a topic here when it answers:

- When you send an invoice or pay a bill
- Who may approve spending
- Where official numbers live, by the name of the screen or folder, not by the login

This folder is for rules an assistant can follow. It is not the place for the numbers themselves.

Leave out bank account numbers, card numbers, routing numbers, tax ID numbers, and payment logins. If a figure is an estimate, label it as an estimate. See [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

Copy a new topic from [`_templates/topic-template.md`](../_templates/topic-template.md).

`example-when-invoices-go-out.md` is a pattern, not your billing rule. Assistants must not cite it.
