# Example: When invoices go out

> **Example only.** This file shows the shape of a topic. It is not your billing rule. Assistants must not cite it. It has no prices and no account numbers on purpose.

## Summary

A real note says when an invoice goes out, in words. A placeholder is: "Invoices go out on the first weekday of the month." Write your own timing, keep account numbers out, and leave the status as draft until you confirm the sentence.

## Details

A finished note can include:

- The day or event that triggers an invoice
- Who is allowed to approve a new charge
- The name of the screen where the official record lives

It does not include a bank account, a card, a tax ID, or a password. If you are unsure of an amount, write **estimate** or leave the amount out.
