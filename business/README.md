# Business

Notes about what the work is.

Put a topic here when it answers:

- What you do, in one sentence
- Who it is for
- What you offer
- What you do not offer, and what an assistant must never promise

Leave out legal filings, tax ID numbers, and home addresses. A tool name is fine. A login is not. See [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

Copy a new topic from [`_templates/topic-template.md`](../_templates/topic-template.md).

`example-what-this-work-is.md` is a pattern, not a real decision. Assistants must not cite it.
