# Example: How messages should sound

> **Example only.** This file shows the shape of a topic. It is not your voice. Assistants must not cite it.

## Summary

A real note tells an assistant how messages should sound and what they must include. A placeholder is: "Use a warm, plain tone. Say what will happen next. Make no promise that is not already written in a confirmed note."

## Details

A finished note can include:

- Words you like, and words you want avoided
- What the first reply to a new person should contain
- Which promises are allowed only after you say yes

Customer secrets do not belong in the details. Store a preference ("they prefer morning calls") and leave out a home address or a card number.
