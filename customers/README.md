# Customers

Notes about how you communicate with the people you serve.

Put a topic here when it answers:

- The tone you want
- What a new customer should know on the first day
- What you will promise, and what you will not promise
- Which customer facts are okay to keep in this repository

Leave out customer passwords, payment details, tax IDs, and home addresses. A first name plus a preference is often enough. See [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

Copy a new topic from [`_templates/topic-template.md`](../_templates/topic-template.md).

`example-how-messages-should-sound.md` is a pattern, not your voice. Assistants must not cite it.
